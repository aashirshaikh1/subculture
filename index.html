Subculture — a microbiology and biotechnology reference, designed, written and built by Aashir Shaikh.
Built for study, not for clinical use. Treatment and dosing decisions belong with a clinician and the current national guideline.
Figures for case numbers, deaths and resistance change every year. Check the cited WHO, CDC and national sources before presenting any of them.
Illustrations and 3D models are schematic — drawn in code to show structure, not to scale and not derived from imaging data.
